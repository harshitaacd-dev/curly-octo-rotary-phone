Pinterest India Transparency Report — April 2026

This report, covering April 2026, includes data categories specified in India IT Rules, 2021. This data reflects submissions and outcomes from Pinterest’s India Grievance Form, where we review content against our Community Guidelines and our Advertising Guidelines. We also include data regarding voluntary actions taken against Indian users' content to enforce our Community Guidelines.

Reports

Reports are submitted through the designated complaint form for grievances or complaints regarding content on Pinterest’s services or compliance with the India IT Rules, 2021. The data in the table below reflects decisions made on individual pieces of content.

Voluntary actions

In addition to reviewing and actioning reported content, content that may violate our guidelines can also be found via voluntary methods. The mechanisms used to detect different potential violations may vary based on the state of available technology, the volume of violative content and other factors such as the complexity of evaluation. We continue to iterate and evolve our tools and expect ongoing advancements as our teams continue to strive to maintain a positive and safe environment. The data in this section covers statistics on deactivations taken on content that was found via voluntary methods, including those actioned using automated tools, against content posted by users located in India.
