3. Removal Actions (Takedown)

We take proactive removal actions basis violation of our Community Guidelines, Terms of Use and other policy standards. All existing policies for Moj can be accessed here.

Our users have the option to report user profiles as well as individual content for violation of our policy standards. We have a reporting mechanism to enable copyright holders to report infringing copyright content on the platform. Such infringing links can be shared for review at https://moj-copyright.sharechat.com/ and are taken down within 24 hours.

4. Removal Actions (Ban)

Depending on the severity of the violation and the parameters basis which each case has been reviewed, we enforce various bans spread across different time periods. The bans can be enforced in the form of UGC ban (user is unable to post any content on the platform for the specified ban duration), edit profile ban (user is unable to edit any profile attributes for the specified time period) and comment ban (user is banned from commenting on any post on the platform for the specified ban duration). The duration of these bans varies between one day, seven days, 30 days and 360 days. In cases where these guidelines are repeatedly breached, we permanently remove the user’s account for a period of 360 days. In the month of February, we permanently terminated 826 user accounts from Moj.
